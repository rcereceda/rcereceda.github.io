Forest Runner: privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Forest Runner is a one-button running game made by Rodrigo Cereceda, an independent developer ("I" below). This page says what the game collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept and how to have it deleted.

In short: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. It sends anonymous play events so I can see whether people come back to it, the nickname and results you choose to share in groups, and, in the Google Play version, what Google's ads need.

What the game sends to my server

  • Play events. When the game opens, when a session starts, when a run starts and when it ends (the mode, the distance and what ended it). Each event carries a random install id made by the game the first time it opens, the game's version, the platform (for example Android or web) and the time. The install id is not your device's id or your advertising id, and nothing in it identifies you. I use these events only to count players, sessions and runs, and to see whether players come back and where runs end, so I can make the game better.
  • Groups, only if you use them. To compare your daily forest runs with friends, the game makes a random player id and a secret key (the server keeps only a hash of the key) and sends the nickname you pick (3 to 10 letters and digits), the names of the groups you create, the groups you join, and the distance and number of tries of your daily runs. The other members of your groups see your nickname and these results. Don't use your real name as a nickname if you'd rather not share it.

My server does not store your IP address, and it does not combine this data with anything else about you. It runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and its D1 database), which handles the connections on my behalf and, like any network provider, sees your IP address while delivering a request (Cloudflare's privacy policy).

Ads (Google Play version only)

The Android version on Google Play shows an occasional full-screen ad between runs, through Google AdMob. The web version and other builds show no ads and include no ad code.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob SDK collects data from your device, such as your device's advertising id, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. This is collected by Google, not by me, and Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If you don't agree, you still get ads, but not personalized ones. In those places you can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button on the title screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising id, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The game asks Google for ads suitable for teens and older.

What stays on your device

Your best distances, settings, the install id, your group player id, key and nickname, and events or runs waiting to be sent while you're offline are saved in the game's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the game (or clearing its data) deletes them; a new install starts with new random ids that are not linked to the old ones.

How long it is kept

  • Play events: 13 months, then deleted.
  • Group data: your daily results are kept for 13 months. Your nickname and player id are kept while you are in a group, and deleted 13 months after you were last active. A group is deleted when its last member leaves.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Leave a group from the game at any time (LEAVE GROUP); the group's creator can also remove members.
  • To have your group data deleted, write to me with your nickname and the name or code of one of your groups. I'll delete your nickname, results and membership.
  • Play events can't be tied to you (I don't know which install id is yours), so I can't pick out yours on request; they are deleted after 13 months, and uninstalling the game stops them.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, or California's CCPA), you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Write to me and I'll answer within 30 days.

I don't sell your data, and I don't share it with anyone other than the providers named on this page.

Children

Forest Runner is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and I don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has shared a nickname in a group, write to me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its date. If the change is significant (for example a new kind of data), I'll say so in the game's update notes.
